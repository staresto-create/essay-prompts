參考範文（約 135 個單詞）

The bar chart shows how elementary and junior high school students in Taiwan are introduced to sports. School PE classes are the most common source, at 68.4%. Classmates come next with 29.4%, and family members follow with 24.9%. In other words, PE classes introduce more than twice as many students to sports as classmates do, while family plays the smallest role.

My own experience matches this pattern. I only played sports in PE classes until a classmate invited me to join the badminton club. Now I practice with my friends twice a week after school. Because PE classes meet only a few times a week, I think schools should open the playground in the afternoon and set up more clubs. When students can exercise with their friends, they are more likely to keep the habit.
