參考範文（約 209 個單詞）

The two charts show how the second-hand prices of a popular blind-box toy series changed between June 10 and June 19, when the company released a large new supply. The first chart focuses on the rarest, hidden figure, whose price fell from 4,699 yuan to 2,048 yuan, a drop of more than half. Even so, it was still about twenty times the official price of 99 yuan. The second chart shows that the ordinary figures were affected as well. Their price range dropped from between 292 and 510 yuan to between 208 and 385 yuan, which was still roughly two to four times the official price.

I think the main reason for the drop is simple: when supply increases, scarcity disappears. Many of the high prices were probably driven by resellers who bought up the toys and sold them for much more. Once ordinary fans could buy the toys at the official price, fewer people needed to pay extra. Personally, I would never pay twenty times the original price for a toy. Limited editions can be exciting, but the feeling of urgency often makes people spend more than they planned. I would rather wait, because trends change fast, and a toy that is everywhere today may be forgotten tomorrow.

這篇範文由本題庫撰寫，不是唯一答案。

  • 第一段要同時處理兩張圖：先各用一句話說明，再用 as well 把共通的變化連起來。
  • 圖上的日期是 2025 年，已經是過去的事，所以描述價格變化要用過去式（fell、dropped）；描述圖本身（The two charts show）用現在式。
  • 原因圖表上看不出來，第二段寫原因要用 I think、probably，不要寫成確定的事實。
