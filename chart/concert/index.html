參考範文（約 195 個單詞）

The bar chart compares how much more Taiwanese travelers spend per day in Japan and South Korea during the ten major concerts with the biggest spending increases and during Taiwan's main holidays than they do on an average day of the year. In Japan, the two figures are close: spending rises by 26% during concerts and by 22% during holidays. In South Korea, however, the gap is striking. Daily spending jumps by 27% during concerts, but only by 7% during holidays, so the concert figure is almost four times as high.

These results do not surprise me. Many of my classmates would happily save their allowance for a ticket to see their favorite band, and some would even fly abroad for a concert. For fans, the experience is worth more than a new pair of shoes, because a concert abroad turns a trip into a special memory and gives them a chance to share the excitement with others. Personally, I would spend money on a concert, too, as long as I paid for it with my own savings. I think it is a better use of money than buying things that I will soon forget.

這篇範文由本題庫撰寫，不是唯一答案。

  • 第一段先講「比較什麼」（compares… than they do on an average day of the year），再分國家寫，最後用 however 帶出最大的差異。
  • 「幾倍」的說法：27 ÷ 7 約 3.9，所以寫 almost four times as high 或 nearly four times as high 都可以，不要寫成 exactly four times 或 more than four times。
  • 第二段先回應圖表（These results…），再寫理由和自己的看法；as long as 引出條件。
